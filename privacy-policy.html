Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026. Short version: DoTo has no server and collects nothing. Your data stays in your browser unless you turn on Google Drive sync or Google Calendar reminders, which talk directly to Google.

1. Data DoTo itself collects

None. There is no analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no DoTo account. The authors of DoTo cannot see your tasks because they never leave your device except as described below.

2. Data stored in your browser

Everything the app needs is kept in your browser's localStorage on your own device:

KeyContents
doto-v1Lists, tasks, subtasks, time records, view, filters, color labels, your name, panel sizes
doto-syncGoogle sync metadata: your email, access token, Drive file id, last-synced snapshot
doto-sync-logRecent sync history (last ~50 events, kept for diagnostics)
doto-cal, doto-cal-queueLinked Google Calendar id and pending calendar deletions
doto-theme, doto-locTheme choice; weather location (7-day cache)

Clearing your browser's site data deletes all of this. Export a JSON backup first (Sync dialog → Export) if it matters to you.

3. Google Drive sync (optional)

  • Only if you sign in with Google: the app requests four scopes — drive.appdata (hidden app folder), userinfo.email (display only), calendar.events (create/edit reminder events) and calendar.calendarlist.readonly (find the target calendar — calendar.events alone cannot list calendars).
  • A copy of your lists/tasks is stored in your own Drive's hidden app folder (doto-state.json). Sync traffic goes directly between your browser and Google's APIs; no intermediary server is involved.
  • Access tokens live about an hour in your browser only. Signing out (or revoking access in your Google account) stops all sync; your local copy stays on the device.

4. Google Calendar reminders (optional)

  • Only tasks where you set Details → Reminder create events, in a "DoTo" calendar in your Google account (or your main calendar if creation isn't permitted).
  • Events contain the task title, notes, list name, due time and popup/email offsets. Completing, deleting or un-setting the reminder deletes the event.

5. Weather card (Home view)

  • The weather card fetches forecasts from Open-Meteo and resolves location via the browser geolocation API with BigDataCloud/ipapi.co fallback. This is the only non-Google network traffic the app makes, and only for that card.
  • Location is cached for 7 days (doto-loc) and never sent anywhere except those weather providers.

6. What never happens

  • No sale, rental or sharing of personal data — there is nothing to share.
  • No cross-site tracking. Fonts and icons ship with the app and load offline.
  • No children's-data collection beyond what any user types into their own tasks.

7. Your control

  • Work fully offline and never sign in — nothing leaves the device at all.
  • Export or delete your data any time (Sync dialog → Export; clearing site data removes everything).
  • Revoke Google access any time in your Google account security settings; already-created calendar events stay in your calendar until you delete them.

8. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated; the date above will change. Questions: open an issue at github.com/arazgray/doto/issues. Also see the Terms of Service and the in-app Manual.
